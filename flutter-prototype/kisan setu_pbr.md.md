Introduction & Business Context### 1.1 About Kisan SetuKisan Setu (किसान सेतु — "Farmer's Bridge") is a super app designed for India's agricultural ecosystem. It currently provides 18 integrated modules covering crop management, mandi (market) prices, equipment rental, transport logistics, financial services, government schemes, and AI-powered advisory — all in Hindi-first UI.### 1.2 Why Multi-Profile NowKisan Setu was built with the farmer as the sole user persona. However, the agricultural supply chain involves multiple distinct actors: landlords who own land but don't farm it, transporters who move produce to markets, traders (vyaparis) who buy and sell at mandis, equipment owners who rent out tractors and harvesters, and brokers (dalals) who facilitate deals between farmers and buyers.Today, all of these users download Kisan Setu and see a farmer-centric interface that is largely irrelevant to their daily workflow. A transporter doesn't need crop advisory. A broker doesn't need water management tools. This mismatch leads to:1. **Low activation rates** among non-farmer users2. **High bounce rates** during onboarding (the app "doesn't feel like it's for me")3. **Feature discoverability problems** — relevant tools are buried among 18 modules4. **Missed monetization opportunities** — transporters, sellers, and brokers represent high-value user segments for premium featuresThe Multi-Profile System solves this by allowing each user to select their primary role, see a curated dashboard, and access only the tools relevant to their work.### 1.3 Business Objectives| Objective | Target Metric | Timeframe |`----------`|---------------|-----------|Increase non-farmer user activation | +40% activation rate | 3 months post-launch |Reduce onboarding drop-off | -25% drop at registration step | 1 month post-launch |Increase per-session tool usage | +30% modules accessed per session | 2 months post-launch |Expand addressable user base | 6 → 6 distinct personas | Launch |---## 2. Problem Statement### Current State- Single user persona (Farmer) serves all users- All 18 modules visible to every user regardless of relevance- No personalization of home screen or navigation- Non-farmer users see irrelevant tools (crop advisory, water management, women farmer programs)- No way for multi-role users (farmer + transporter) to switch context### Desired State- Six distinct user personas with curated experiences- Each persona sees only relevant modules in the tools drawer- Persona-specific home dashboard with role-relevant widgets- Seamless profile switching for multi-role users- Profile selection during first-time onboarding- Persistent profile choice across app sessions### ScopeThis PBR covers the client-side implementation only. Backend profile sync, profile-specific content APIs, and team/organization accounts are deferred to Phase 2+ (see Section 9).---## 3. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria### US-1: First-Time User Selects Profile**As a** first-time user**I want to** choose my role (farmer, transporter, seller, etc.) during sign-up**So that** I see only the tools relevant to my work from day one**Acceptance Criteria:**- [ ] AC-1: After selecting Hindi/English language, I see a "Choose Your Profile" screen with 6 cards- [ ] AC-2: Each card shows an icon, Hindi name, English name, and Hindi tagline- [ ] AC-3: I can select only one card at a time- [ ] AC-4: The "Continue" button is disabled until I select a card- [ ] AC-5: After selecting and tapping Continue, I proceed to phone registration- [ ] AC-6: After completing registration, I land on the home view for my selected profile---### US-2: Transporter Switches to Seller Profile**As a** user who is both a transporter and a seller**I want to** switch between my profiles without re-logging in**So that** I can manage both businesses from one app**Acceptance Criteria:**- [ ] AC-1: I tap my avatar in the top bar and see a dropdown menu- [ ] AC-2: The dropdown shows my active profile badge and a list of linked profiles- [ ] AC-3: I tap "व्यापारी" (Seller) and the app immediately shows the Seller Home- [ ] AC-4: The tools drawer now shows only seller-relevant modules- [ ] AC-5: A toast confirms: "प्रोफाइल बदली: व्यापारी"---### US-3: Farmer Sees No Change**As an** existing farmer user**I want to** my app experience to remain exactly the same**So that** I don't have to relearn anything**Acceptance Criteria:**- [ ] AC-1: After updating the app, I see the same home screen with all 18 modules- [ ] AC-2: All existing functionality works identically- [ ] AC-3: I can optionally add other profiles via the avatar menu- [ ] AC-4: Adding a profile does not change my farmer home view---### US-4: User Adds a Second Profile**As a** farmer who also runs a transport business**I want to** add a transporter profile to my account**So that** I can switch to transport tools when needed**Acceptance Criteria:**- [ ] AC-1: I tap my avatar → "नई प्रोफाइल जोड़ें" (Add New Profile)- [ ] AC-2: A bottom sheet shows all 6 profiles; Farmer shows a check mark (already linked)- [ ] AC-3: I tap "परिवहन" (Transport) and it's added to my linked profiles- [ ] AC-4: I can now see Transport in my profile switcher dropdown---### US-5: User Cannot Access Restricted Routes**As a** transporter user**I want to** be prevented from navigating to farmer-only screens**So that** I'm not confused by irrelevant features**Acceptance Criteria:**- [ ] AC-1: If I try to navigate to "advisory" (a farmer-only route), a toast appears: "इस प्रोफाइल में यह स्क्रीन उपलब्ध नहीं है"- [ ] AC-2: The tools drawer does not show farmer-only modules- [ ] AC-3: The farmer-only modules are completely hidden, not just greyed out---### US-6: Profile Persists Across App Restarts**As a** transporter user**I want to** stay logged in as a transporter when I close and reopen the app**So that** I don't have to re-select my profile every time**Acceptance Criteria:**- [ ] AC-1: I close and reopen Kisan Setu- [ ] AC-2: The app opens directly to the Transport Home (skipping onboarding)- [ ] AC-3: My tools drawer shows transport-relevant modules---## 7.
