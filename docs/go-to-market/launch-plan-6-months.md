# AGROVERCITY (Kisan Setu) — Go-To-Market: Two-Phase Plan (Fill → Earn)

**Model:** Phase A = free growth to 10 lakh downloads with a liquid marketplace → Phase B = turn on ₹99 Pro and convert to 1 lakh payers ≈ ₹99 lakh MRR (~₹1 crore/month).
**Timeline:** Phase 0 = 3 weeks from Oct 5, 2026 · Phase A (Fill) = M1 (Diwali, Nov 2026) → M11 · Phase B (Earn) = paywall on M12 → 1 lakh payers ~M15.
**Budget:** ₹1L/month founder cash (M1 onward) + 80% of voluntary-Pro revenue reinvested; fully subscription-funded from M12.
**Channels:** Google + Meta ads, organic content/SEO, WhatsApp, and B2B2C institutional onboarding. No government reliance. No commissions.
**Pricing:** Flat **₹99/month per persona**. Free tier (mandi prices, khata, diary) free forever.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The founder directive: **users will not pay until the platform is full.** Sequence: ~10 lakh free downloads first, then monetize to 1 lakh paying subscribers at ₹99. This document rebuilds the go-to-market around that order.

Why this order is not just acceptable but *correct* for a 20-persona marketplace: a transporter who opens an empty load board, or a vyapari who sees zero farmer listings, churns in a day and never returns. A paying user acquired into an empty marketplace is money burned twice (CAC + churn). A free user acquired into a *filling* marketplace becomes the liquidity that future payers will pay to reach.

**The strategy in one line: every persona downloads for a free tool that works alone, stays for the marketplace that fills district by district, and pays ₹99 only once their customers/suppliers are visibly inside.**

Three mechanisms make the cold start solvable:

1. **Tool-first, network-second** — each of the 20 personas gets standalone utility that works with zero other users (khata, trip P&L, FAT/SNF calculator, mandi prices). This is what makes 10 lakh free downloads possible at all.
2. **District-first liquidity** — the marketplace is not one marketplace; it is ~100 district marketplaces built one at a time. Nashik is flooded with farmers *before* a single transporter is recruited in Nashik.
3. **Seeded supply** — B2B2C bulk onboarding (dairy societies, FPOs, transport unions = 50–500 users per partner), ops-posted "verified" listings, and concierge matching keep every board alive from day one.

**Headline trajectory (planning targets; assumptions and sensitivities in §5):**

| Milestone | When |
|---|---|
| 10 lakh downloads + liquidity gates met | M10–M11 |
| Paywall ON (₹99 Pro, founding-member lock) | M12 |
| ₹1 crore/month subscription MRR | M14–M15 (~1 lakh payers) |
| ₹2 crore/month total revenue | M18–M20 |
| ₹20 crore/month | M30–36 (scale phase, §12) |

## 2. The Cold-Start Doctrine (why this works with zero data)

### 2.1 The problem, stated plainly

Every persona's demand derives from another persona: the transporter needs farmers/vyapari posting loads; the vyapari needs farmer supply and buyer demand; the direct buyer needs farmer lots; the vet, instructor, SHG all need farmers as clients. **The farmer is the atomic counterparty** — every load, listing, booking, consult, and loan in the system starts with farmer activity. And liquidity is *local*: a Nashik transporter does not care about loads in Indore.

Therefore the bootstrap sequence is fixed and non-negotiable: **farmer density first → business counterparties on top of farmer-dense districts → services/finance personas last.** Never market a counterparty persona into a district before its counterparties exist there.

### 2.2 Rule 1 — Tool first, network second

Every persona gets a free standalone tool that delivers value on day one with zero other users. People download for the tool; they stay for the network; they pay for capacity. (The Khatabook/Meesho playbook.)

| 2 | seller/vyapari | Digital khata, invoicing, inventory, J-form, 20 free listings | Farmer listings flowing in; buyer demands. Recruit after district ≥10k farmer MAU. |
| 3 | transporter | Trip P&L, diesel/expense log, GST bilty, 1 vehicle free | Load-board bids from farmer/vyapari loads. Recruit after ≥10k farmer MAU + ops-seeded loads live. |

### 2.3 Rule 2 — Farmer first, district by district

We build district marketplaces, not a national one. The unit of growth is a **liquid district**, and expansion is gated on district metrics — never on global download count.

**District playbook (repeat per district):**

1. **Flood** — farmer free-tool acquisition until the district crosses **≥10,000 farmer MAU** (Nashik first, then Pune, Ahmednagar, Jalgaon, Kolhapur, Sambhajinagar, then Vidarbha).
2. **Attach** — recruit vyapari → transporter → equipmentOwner *inside that district only*. Each one opens the app and immediately sees live farmer listings/loads. This is the moment the marketplace pitch becomes true.
3. **Seed** — ops team posts "Agrovercity Verified" loads/listings and runs concierge matching (phone/WhatsApp) so early users experience "it works" even at thin volume.
4. **Deepen** — dairy societies, brokers, landlords, SHG wave into the same district per the §2.2 thresholds.
5. **Clone** — once the district sustains liquidity for 4 consecutive weeks, open the next district and repeat.

**Liquidity gates (dashboard-tracked per district, §11):** farmer MAU ≥10k · active listings ≥200 · loads matched ≥40/week · machine bookings ≥15/week · dairy members registered ≥500. A district is *liquid* when all five hold for 4 straight weeks.

### 2.4 Rule 3 — Seed the supply by hand until the flywheel spins

Phase A is deliberately ops-heavy and cash-light:

- **B2B2C bulk onboarding:** one dairy society ≈ 150 members, one FPO ≈ 300 farmers, one transport union ≈ 100 truckers, one custom-hiring centre ≈ 50 machines. Target: 50 partner institutions by M3, 200 by M6, 500 by M9. One partner signature = more users than a week of ads.
- **Physical presence:** founder/outreach team sits at mandis, milk collection centres, truck stands, haats in active districts — registering users by hand, 30–50/day.
- **Public data seeding:** Agmarknet mandi prices, weather, crop advisory imported so the app is never empty even at user count zero.
- **Ops-posted verified listings/loads:** clearly marked, real (sourced from partner institutions and mandi floor visits) — the boards look alive from day one.
- **Concierge matching:** for the first ~90 days per district, humans match loads/deals behind the app. The software formalizes what the ops team already delivers by hand.
- **Liquidity windows:** scheduled match events that concentrate thin supply and demand into the same moment — daily 6pm load-board refresh, weekly SHG bazaar, harvest-season equipment slot auctions.

### 2.5 Rule 4 — The free majority IS the product for the paying minority

At monetization, ~9 of 10 users will still be free. That is not a bug — the 9 lakh free users are the marketplace liquidity the 1 lakh payers pay ₹99 to reach. Therefore: **the free tier (mandi prices, khata, diary, browsing) stays free forever, and Phase B monetizes capacity and business features, never the base utility.** Charging early kills the very asset we later sell.

## 3. Revenue Model

### 3.1 Phase A (M1 → M11): free, funded by the Voluntary Pro hybrid

Everything is free. Nothing is gated. **₹99 is never mentioned in any ad, video, or caption.** But the Pro plan exists in-app: when a power user hits a free-tier wall (seller's 21st listing, transporter's 2nd vehicle, dairy's 26th member), the paywall offers ₹99/month to unlock. This is the **voluntary-Pro hybrid** — the default funding path:

- Users who are earning through the app self-select into paying; nobody is pushed.
- Expected trickle: ~300 payers by M3 → ~2.5k by M6 → ~10–12k by M10–M11 (~₹10–12L/month by paywall time). 80% reinvested into acquisition.
- Product work: the paywall moment must be excellent — in-context ("aapki 21vi listing — Pro mein unlimited"), one-tap UPI, 15-day free unlock code for hesitators.

**Founder's funding decision (the one fork in this plan):**

| Path | Monthly input | Time to 10L downloads | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B. Voluntary Pro hybrid (DEFAULT)** | ₹1L cash + 80% of voluntary revenue | **~11 months** | Keeps "free first" promise; self-funding; no investors. This plan models Path B. |
| A. Pure free — ₹1L cash only | ₹1L | ~30–36 months | Strictest "nobody pays" reading; organic must carry almost everything. |
| C. War chest ₹60L–1cr | ₹8–12L | ~6–8 months | Angels / agri incubators / NABARD-type schemes. Fastest; dilution + raise effort. |
| D. Founder injects ₹5–10L/mo | ₹5–10L | ~12–15 months | No investors; highest personal exposure. |

Fallback rule: if voluntary revenue underperforms plan, budgets automatically shrink to cash-available and the timeline stretches toward Path A — **never buy downloads ahead of liquidity.**

### 3.2 Phase B (M12 onward): ₹99 Pro + on-platform ads

**Trigger (all three, verified 4 consecutive weeks):** 10L cumulative downloads AND ≥4L MAU AND ≥10 liquid districts.

**The switch:**
1. **T-60 to T-45 days:** announcement everywhere — "Agrovercity Pro launches [festival date]. Business tools ₹99/month. First 1 lakh members lock lifetime ₹99 + founding-member badge + 3 months free." The announcement itself drives a download surge (join before the gate).
2. **Grandfathering:** all existing users get founding-lock pricing (₹99 never increases for them); early voluntary payers get 3 bonus months free.
3. **T-day (M12):** paywall on. Free tier unchanged (mandi prices, khata, diary, browsing stay free). Capacity + business features go Pro, per the persona limit table in §16.

**Phase B revenue lines:**
- **Subscriptions:** flat ₹99/month per persona, UPI + AutoPay. Ramp: ~2–3% of MAU converts in month one, climbing to ~10% by month 4–6 of Phase B → **~1 lakh payers ≈ ₹99L MRR (~₹1 crore/month) around M15.**
- **On-platform ads** (opens M13, once the audience has proven it pays): sponsored listings ₹99/7 days, featured shop ₹499/30 days, boosted loads ₹49, course promotion ₹199, banners ₹999/week. Fill ramp: M13 ₹50k → M15 ₹3L → M18 ₹7L/month.

### 3.3 What we deliberately do NOT monetize (founder decision, unchanged)

- **No transaction commission** — transport/equipment/broker take-rates stay off.
- **No government scheme / DBT dependency.**
- **No paywall on the free-tier basics** — monetize capacity, not survival utility.

## 5. Unit Economics & Assumptions (the honest math)

| Assumption | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Blended CAC (paid) per signup | ₹12 | Rural Meta CPC ₹0.5–2, landing conversion ~12%; exact/phrase match only |
| Referral multiplier on paid signups | 1.55× | `referrals.py` + AgriCoins (zero cash cost); WhatsApp share loops |
| Organic share of total signups | **≥50% required** | 1 Short + 1 Reel/day, mandi-price SEO, WhatsApp virality, **B2B2C bulk** |
| Voluntary Pro (Phase A) | 0.3k payers M3 → 2.5k M6 → 12k M11 | Un-marketed; power B2B users hitting limits; in-context paywall |
| Free → paid (Phase B) | 2–3% in paywall month 1 → ~10% by month 4–6 | Business personas convert 15–25%, farmers 5–7%; AutoPay + daily utility |
| Paying churn | 5%/month | Dunning + pause-instead-of-cancel + win-back offers |
| Founder cash | ₹1L/month throughout | Path B default |

**Critical dependency:** if organic+B2B2C delivers only 25% of signups instead of 50%, 10L downloads slips from M10–M11 to ~M15 and the paywall to ~M16. The daily rhythm (§10) therefore protects the organic engine as fiercely as the ad budget. Path B never buys its way out of an organic failure.

## 11. KPI Dashboard & Operating Rules

**Daily dashboard (one sheet):** per district — signups, farmer MAU, listings, loads matched/week, bookings, dairy members (the five gates) · activation % (Day-1: mandi alert set OR first khata/diary/listing entry) · referral share · spend/CAC by campaign and district · (Phase B) paywall views, purchases, free→paid %, MRR, renewals due/paid, churn, ads-product revenue.

**North star:** Phase A = **weekly new users in active districts** (downloads outside open districts count for nothing) · Phase B = **weekly new payers (₹99)**.

**Kill/scale rules (non-negotiable):**
- Ad set CAC > ₹20 for 3 days → kill; < ₹12 for 3 days → +30% every 3 days (max 2×/week).
- Meta creative frequency > 3 → refresh.
- Activation < 40% for 2 weeks → pause acquisition, fix onboarding first (filling a leaking lake is burning money).
- **District gate rule:** a counterparty persona's campaign may not launch in a district until that district's farmer-MAU gate is crossed — enforced in the media plan, not just in intent.
- (Phase B) Free→paid < 6% for 4 weeks → pause acquisition, fix paywall/value story.
- (Phase B) Renewal < 80% → freeze budget growth; churn war-room.
- Any district with all gates green 4 weeks → clone its campaign to the next-nearest district.

**Funding rule (Path B):** monthly ad budget = ₹1L cash + 80% of trailing voluntary/subscription revenue. Never spend ahead of liquidity or revenue.
